Vacation Rental Furnishing From Competitive Analysis to Setup

We take care of your vacation rental furnishing as one complete job, starting with competitive analysis and moving through design to final setup.

Your property, budget and goals shape a coordinated plan for furniture and amenities, so your spending goes toward the strongest possible return instead of isolated purchases.

How our furnishing service runs from start to finish.

  1. Learn your property and goals

    We get to know your property, your needs, your budget and your goals.

  2. Study nearby competition

    We look at the vacation rentals near you.

  3. Build the furnishing plan

    We build a furnishing plan and amenity choices that make your furniture spending go further, with a budget aimed at the strongest return.

  4. Final setup

    The home is furnished and set up, ready for guests.

Getting Started: The Measurements and Walkthrough We Need

Since we design remotely, we need a floor plan or room-by-room measurements from you, along with a video walkthrough and the width of every doorway, hallway, stairway and elevator on the delivery route.

Measure the whole route before design starts, not just the rooms. That includes tight stair turns, landings and elevator depth. If anything is missing, we book a local partner to measure the home and confirm delivery clearances within 7 days, before anything is ordered.

The partner bills you directly at its own rate. If you're unsure of your measurements, booking the partner means you aren't working from uncertain numbers.

Measuring yourself saves that fee, but any mistake found on delivery day is yours: furniture that won't fit through becomes a return, a delay and a gap in the finished home.

Review and Approve Your Design Board Before Anything Is Ordered

Nothing gets ordered until you've reviewed and approved the room designs, layouts and purchasing details. This is the cheapest moment to catch a problem, because once items are ordered, any change means returns, restocking and a later launch.

What you approve before anything is ordered.

  1. Review the design board

    See the look, colors and key pieces for each room.

  2. Check the layout plan

    Check the furnished layout for every room against how guests will move through and use it.

  3. Check the itemized list

    Check each item's supplier, price and lead time against your launch date.

  4. Sign off in writing

    Approve the board and layout and sign off on the itemized list in writing.

  5. Ordering begins

    Only after your written sign-off is anything ordered.

What Shapes Your Airbnb Furnishing Cost

  • Property size

    The number of bedrooms and the square footage set how many pieces it takes to furnish your home.

  • Finish selection

    You choose from 3 finish tiers (essential, mid-range or premium), and that choice affects each room's cost more than anything else.

  • Market fit

    We match the tier to comparable nightly rates and what guests expect, not to personal taste. Finishes below premium cut your upfront and replacement costs but lose appeal with top-market guests.

  • Customization and existing furniture

    Once your tier is set, custom or made-to-order pieces raise the total, and keeping furniture you already own brings it down.

  • Amenity extras

    Furnishings for a pool, hot tub or game room add to the total on top of your finish tier.

  • Written quote

    We send a written quote within 3 business days of receiving your property details, itemizing these choices separately from our management fee.

  • Supplier pricing

    Furniture suppliers invoice you directly at their own prices, and we put no markup on those purchases.

  • Listing photography

    Through Airbnb Listing Optimization & SEO, we arrange an optional professional photo shoot.

Interior Design Upgrades That Lift Occupancy and Nightly Rates

Demand-led improvements

Before any money goes on furnishing, we look at local guest demand and recommend design and amenity changes that target higher occupancy and nightly rates, based on what guests expect and how they book.

Amenities before décor

Pools, hot tubs and experience-driven spaces come first for us, because your money goes further on features guests search for and pay for than on décor alone.

Upgrades and remodeling

We advise you on amenities, upgrades and repositioning. When the home needs interior design or remodeling work, we source certified designers, architects and licensed contractors through our partner network.

Project timing

A full furnishing project takes about 6–8 weeks, depending on the size of the property, how much you customize and what's available. Customization, upgrades and remodeling make the project longer than furnishing the home as it stands.

Rental conversion support

If you're converting a home into a short-term rental, we support you from choosing the property and furnishing it through setup and ongoing management.

Our management role

We're a short-term rental management and co-hosting company that runs rental listings remotely for property owners.

Guest-Proof Furniture, Safety Essentials and Amenities

  • Guest-proof furniture

    We pick durable, easy-to-clean pieces with stain-resistant finishes and fabrics that stand up to constant turnover, so you replace less between stays. The trade-off is that you get fewer delicate or statement designs than residential furniture offers.

  • Tip-over-resistant storage

    We select dressers and storage units that meet ASTM F2057 for tip-over resistance, to protect your guests.

  • Bunk bed safety

    The bunk beds we choose meet ASTM F1427, with guardrails on both sides of the top bunk and a fixed ladder. Guests under 6 don't use the top bunk.

  • Anchoring and clear exits

    Our delivery partners anchor tall furniture and wall-mounted TVs and keep a clear path at least 36 inches wide to every door and egress window, so no furniture blocks an exit.

  • Smoke and carbon monoxide

    Our local partners put smoke alarms in each bedroom and on every level. They add carbon monoxide alarms wherever the home has gas appliances or an attached garage.

  • Emergency essentials

    Every furnishing setup comes with at least one fire extinguisher in the kitchen and a stocked first-aid kit, installed by our local partners.

  • Local safety requirements

    Before setup, we check your city, state and platform rules for short-term rental safety and add any equipment they require, such as evacuation maps or extinguishers on each floor. This protects both your guests and your listing.

  • Amenities and move-in kits

    We stock kitchenware, board games and pool toys through a supply partner, and our move-in kits cover kitchen essentials, linens and towels.

Smart Locks, Electronics and Streaming Setup

  • Local installation

    Our local partners install and set up smart locks, TVs, streaming devices such as Apple TV, routers with working internet, games, coffee makers, grills and other guest amenities.

  • Existing device eligibility

    We keep and configure your smart locks, routers and automation hubs only if their app supports remote code management and they still receive security updates. If they don't, we recommend replacing them.

  • Secure guest access

    A unique code for each guest and up-to-date firmware keep access secure and manageable, and nobody has to visit the property.

  • Retained hardware trade-off

    Keeping eligible devices saves you the cost of new hardware, but you give up the features and compatibility that newer devices offer.

  • Lock fit checks

    If a lock doesn't match your door, frame, latch bore, strike plate or deadbolt setup, we recommend replacing it. Before we start, we quote the hardware and any locksmith or handyman work it needs.

  • Accounts and billing

    Your device, internet, streaming and subscription accounts are in your name, with you as administrator. Sellers bill you directly, and we work through the access you grant us.

  • Credential handover

    Within 1 business day of setup, we send you every login and administrator credential for your devices and accounts.

  • Access removal

    When our engagement ends, we remove our own access to your devices within 1 business day.

White Glove Delivery, Assembly and Final Walkthrough

  1. Source and deliver

    Our white glove service takes care of sourcing, shipping and delivering all furniture and amenities to your home. Furniture usually arrives within 2-3 weeks; we chase any supplier delay and tell you.

  2. Inspect and assemble

    When items arrive, our delivery partners inspect every one and photograph any damage. Then they assemble everything and clean up afterwards.

  3. Resolve supplier claims

    We handle returns, replacements and warranty claims, including defects that show up after installation within the supplier’s warranty period. The purchases, warranties and refunds stay yours, and we send you the claim records within 2 business days of filing.

  4. Test the home

    Once assembly is done, our local partner tests every item against the itemized list you approved, including lock codes, Wi-Fi, TVs, appliances and alarms.

  5. Report and accept

    Within 2 business days of the walkthrough, we send you dated photos of every room, the checked-off item list and the open issues with their fix dates. You accept the home once those issues are closed.

  6. Open your calendar

    You open your calendar after the walkthrough, once the issues are closed. Waiting means your first booking comes later than if you opened on delivery. But missing items, dead TVs and faulty codes lead to early reviews, and those hurt new listings most.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you furnish an Airbnb?

We start with your property, budget and goals, look at comparable rentals nearby and build a furnishing plan that stretches your spending further. We choose durable, guest-proof furniture, along with kitchenware, board games, pool toys and move-in kits covering kitchen essentials, linens and towels. Our delivery partners then source, ship, deliver and assemble everything and clean up afterwards, so the home is ready for guests.

Can I hire you just to design and furnish the property, without signing up for ongoing management?

Yes. You can hire us for design and furnishing on their own, with no ongoing management. We quote the work in writing and you agree it directly with our team. Once the home is furnished, you can add co-hosting or full management.

Do I have to pay for all the furniture upfront, or is payment split across stages of the project?

Payment is staged, so you don't pay for everything upfront. As each batch is ordered, suppliers, device sellers and installers bill you directly at their own prices, with no markup from us. Our furnishing project fee is split in two: 50% when you approve the written quote and 50% after final setup, each due within 7 days.

How many design revision rounds are included before additional design work becomes chargeable?

Your furnishing plan includes 2 rounds of revisions after the first concept. If you want a third round or more, we quote that extra work in writing before we start it.

Can you phase a furnishing refresh between existing bookings, or must the property be taken off the rental calendar?

Yes. We phase refreshes into vacant windows of at least 2 nights and block only those dates, so confirmed stays go ahead. Phasing protects your existing stays and reviews, but it takes longer, and old and new décor sit side by side for a while. When more than 2 rooms are being fully refurnished at once, we use a single approved block of 5–7 days in a lower-demand period. That finishes faster and means no guest stays with partly furnished rooms, but it costs you bookable nights.

Do I need to be at the property for delivery and assembly, and what do I need to arrange with my HOA or building?

No. You give us access details, such as a lockbox or smart lock code, and we coordinate the delivery partners on the day. If the home is in an HOA or managed building, you arrange the move-in approval, any required insurance certificate and the elevator or loading dock booking at least 5 business days ahead. We give you the delivery window and partner details to include in your submission.

My cabin is in a remote area. Can your delivery partners still deliver and assemble everything there?

Yes. We furnish homes in the United States and Canada, remote cabins included, and elsewhere where our partners can deliver. Where our delivery partners don't have coverage, we coordinate your existing local team or source and vet a new local company within 14 days. For unpaved roads, steep drives or sites a truck can't reach, we plan for a smaller vehicle or a larger crew. Remote delivery adds 1–2 weeks to the usual 2–3 weeks it takes furniture to arrive.

Can you coordinate the furnishing project using suppliers or installers I already have under contract?

Yes. We schedule the suppliers and installers you already have under contract alongside our delivery partners. We vouch only for work done by partners we arrange; your own contractors' workmanship stays covered by your contract with them.
