About Our Company and How We Work

We manage and co-host short-term rentals for property owners, and we do it remotely. If any of our work isn't up to standard, our terms commit us to putting it right.

After that, you can cancel with no fee on 30 days' notice, and we hand over within 5 business days.

What we do if our service falls short of your agreement

  1. Partner work redone or replaced

    If a clean or repair we arrange falls short of the standards in your management agreement, the partner redoes it at no extra cost to you, or we replace them.

  2. Our work fixed

    If you report a problem with our own work, we fix it within 2 business days.

  3. Cancel without a fee

    If we still don't meet your expectations, you can cancel with 30 days' notice and pay no cancellation fee.

  4. Handover file sent

    We send your handover file within 5 business days.

Key Terms, Fees and Partner Work

  • Your platform accounts

    We look after your listing, pricing, calendar, guest messages and reviews inside your own platform accounts. Our remote team has no staff at your property.

  • Partners and clients

    We arrange local partners to handle cleaning, key handover and maintenance. We work directly with owners and hosts rather than guests, and we don't sell or book stays.

  • Your management agreement

    The management agreement you sign with our registered US company holds the full terms; this page summarizes the key ones.

  • Fees and invoicing

    Full management costs 10% of rental revenue plus a monthly software fee per listing, invoiced with your statement by the 10th and payable within 7 days by ACH or card.

  • After you cancel

    We invoice our 10% fee only on stays that check out by your end date, plus the software fee for the final month.

  • Partner work and repairs

    Local partners are independent companies liable for their own work and invoice you directly with no markup; repairs above our approval limit need your written approval.
