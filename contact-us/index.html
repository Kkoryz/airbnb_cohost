Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit your office to meet someone in person, and where is it?

We work fully online, so we don't hold in-person office meetings. Instead, you meet our team on a video discovery call, which we schedule within 3 business days of our reply. We have no staff of our own at any property. You sign every engagement with our registered US company, and its full legal name and business address appear in your management agreement and on every invoice. That address tells you which company you're contracting with. It isn't an office you can visit for a meeting.

How soon will I get a reply after I send a message?

We reply to every enquiry within one business day. The enquiry form gets replies during business hours, so it isn't the way to get immediate help with an urgent issue at a property where guests are staying.

Who do I contact if something urgent happens at my property outside office hours?

Call your dedicated client support line. Outside business hours, the line is for urgent issues at a property where guests are staying, and our support team handles those 24/7. As a client, you also have your own text line, and you never wait on hold.

Can I write for a relative who owns the property, or does the owner have to contact you directly?

Yes, you can send an enquiry for a relative. Before we go ahead with management, we confirm that the person engaging us owns the property or is authorized to manage it. Being family doesn't replace that ownership or authority requirement. We take on properties worldwide, mainly in the United States and Canada, and manage them fully remotely, wherever local rules allow short-term rentals. We don't accept properties in places where short-term rentals are prohibited. Before we accept a listing, we check the local rules, including any permit or licensed-manager requirement. We also review its revenue potential using the nightly rates, occupancy and seasonality of comparable local listings over the past 12 months. These checks confirm whether the property can legally operate and show what it can earn before we take it on.
