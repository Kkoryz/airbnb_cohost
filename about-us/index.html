About Staylumen: Who We Are and the Owners We Work For

Owners worldwide, mainly in the US and Canada, work with one remote team that answers to a registered US company.

Where We're Registered and the Owners We Serve Worldwide

We manage short-term rental listings for owners worldwide, mainly in the United States and Canada, and the whole service runs remotely. We're a registered US company. You'll see our full legal name and business address on your management agreement and on every invoice.

Who We Help: Property Owners and Hosts, Not Guests

For owners and hosts

Our short-term rental management and co-hosting service is for property owners and hosts, and you agree every engagement directly with our team.

Not a guest booking service

We don't sell or book stays for guests. Our job is to run your rental listings for you.

Remote by Design, Backed by Local Partners and a Proven Hosting Record

  • Online operations

    We run your listing, pricing, calendar, guest messages and reviews inside your own platform accounts. We have no staff of our own at any property.

  • Local property support

    We arrange vetted local partner companies to handle cleaning, key handover and maintenance at your property, and we coordinate their work.

  • Credentials you can check

    We hold Airbnb Superhost and Vrbo Premier Host status. If you ask, we'll send you our host profile links so you can verify them before you sign.

  • Consistent property setup

    Every property goes through the same standard processes and accountability checks. Our systems are repeatable, so performance stays consistent as you grow from one property to several.

  • Owner reporting preview

    On the discovery call, we'll show you a sample monthly owner statement with the personal details removed.

Meet the Specialists Behind Your Listings

Our central team is made up of 12 specialists, and each one focuses on a particular part of running your listing. They work across many properties, which helps them spot and fix problems faster than a single generalist would.

You work with us as the online operator of your listing.

How Many Listings We Manage and the Results We Can Show You

On the discovery call, we'll confirm how many listings we currently manage and show you anonymized results for comparable properties in our care, measured over the 12 months before we took over and the 12 months after.

Results from similar properties in a similar market give you a more realistic picture for your listing than a headline average does.

Our Insurance and What It Covers

Our business insurance is separate from your property and short-term rental cover: our business policy doesn't replace the host insurance your property needs.

We carry general and professional liability insurance for our remote management work, which we're liable for.

Within 2 business days of your request, we'll send our certificate of insurance showing coverage types, limits and exclusions for you to check. Each local partner is an independent company liable for its own on-site work, so claims from that work fall under its insurance, not ours.

Ask about each partner's cover separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will one named person look after my property, or will I deal with whoever on the team is free?

Your property gets a named account manager as your main contact for listing, pricing, calendar and guest issues, plus a named backup who covers absences. You contact your account manager directly and get a reply within one business day. With one accountable contact, issues don't get lost.

Do you run rentals of your own that compete with mine for the same guests?

No. We don't own, rent out or book stays in any properties of our own. Every listing belongs to an owner and runs in that owner's platform accounts, so the reviews and history stay with the owner. Where we manage several listings in the same market, each one gets its own property-specific pricing and calendar strategy.

How long have you been managing short-term rentals for owners?

We have more than 10 years of hosting experience, and today we manage over 110 properties for owners.

Is the cleaning and maintenance done by your own staff or by outside contractors?

Cleaning, key handover and maintenance are handled by outside local partner companies. We arrange and coordinate their work for you; we have no staff of our own at your property.
