Who We Are and How We Access Your Accounts

You never share your platform passwords with us: you add us through each platform’s co-host or team-access feature on Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com or another platform, and you can limit or remove our access at any time.

When the engagement ends, we remove our access within 1 business day. We use your account, payout and guest data only to run your listing. Only the team members working on your account can access it.

We never sell or share it for marketing, and we delete any copies we hold within 30 days of the engagement ending.

How We Protect Guest ID and Personal Data

  • Platform verification

    For platform bookings, we rely on each booking platform’s own guest ID verification before a booking is confirmed.

  • Identity document handling

    We never collect guest identity documents outside the booking platforms, and we never download ID images.

  • Owner document collection

    For platform bookings, the platforms collect guest ID, so you don't have to gather or store guests' identity documents yourself.

  • Platform security controls

    For platform bookings, guests’ personal details and ID data stay inside the booking platforms, where the platforms' own security and compliance controls protect them.

  • Direct and returning stays

    For direct and returning-guest stays, guest details reach us through your direct-booking website or from you, payments go to an account in your name, and platform ID checks don't apply.
