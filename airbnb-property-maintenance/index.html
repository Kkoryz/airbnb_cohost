What Our Airbnb Property Maintenance Covers

We coordinate both one-off repairs and ongoing Airbnb property maintenance through local partners, and we handle every repair job on your behalf.

That work covers prevention, response and compliance. For HVAC, electrical and plumbing work, we bring in licensed, vetted local professionals, not general handymen. We don't have staff at your property, and we don't do any hands-on work ourselves.

You contract renovations, remodels, structural repairs and any other project that needs a building permit separately.

Repair Coordination Through Your Home Warranty

If you have a home warranty, we send covered repairs through your provider first. We place the service request and arrange access for the technician. We use our vetted local partners for work the warranty excludes.

We also use them for emergencies that threaten safety or cause ongoing damage and can't wait for the warranty technician.

Remote Maintenance Coordination and Fees

  • Partner invoices

    Local partners bill you directly at their own maintenance rates, and we add no markup.

  • Coordination fees

    Arranging repairs and following up on them are covered by our 10% remote management fee and our USD 100 monthly software fee per listing. There's no separate charge for coordination.

Repair Approval Limits and Emergency Spending

We approve routine repairs up to USD 300 per job.

For anything above that, partners wait for your written approval. If you want tighter control over spending, ask us in writing to get your approval on smaller jobs as well.

If an emergency threatens safety or causes ongoing damage and we can't reach you, we authorize up to USD 1,000 and notify you within 1 hour.

Preventive Maintenance and Turnover Inspections

Our partners inspect your property at every turnover. They also check the major systems separately on a fixed preventive schedule.

Monthly checks

Each month, partners test the smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, check the fire extinguishers and replace HVAC filters as needed.

Quarterly checks

Every quarter, as part of your preventive maintenance schedule, partners test every appliance, the GFCI outlets, the smart locks and the thermostats.

Seasonal servicing

Twice a year, before the cooling and heating seasons, partners arrange full HVAC servicing. They also check the water heater, roof, gutters and any pool or hot tub equipment.

Turnover damage checks

At every turnover, partners look over your walls, floors, furniture, fixtures and outdoor areas for damage and leaks.

Turnover functional tests

Partners test the lights, locks and smart locks, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, thermostat and HVAC, hot water, every appliance, the TV and the Wi-Fi.

Specialist follow-up

If partners find a problem inside an HVAC unit, electrical panel, gas appliance, water heater or pool and hot tub equipment, we book a separate visit from a licensed specialist.

Inspection reports

Within 24 hours of each visit, we send you the condition of every item checked, with dated photos and recommended repairs. This covers filters, thermostats, detectors, appliances, plumbing and smart locks.

Maintenance history

We log every inspection and repair with its date, partner and invoice. You can ask for the full log anytime or review each month's entries alongside your statement.

24/7 Emergency Repair Response During Guest Stays

We coordinate emergencies 24/7 and handle guest communication while repairs are underway.

  • Immediate callouts

    A safety threat, ongoing damage or an unusable property means we send a partner out immediately. That includes active leaks, loss of heat or AC, power outages and lockouts.

  • Habitability response

    For urgent leaks, AC failures and lockouts, we aim to have a local partner there within 4 hours, and if none can attend, we book another local vendor.

  • Minor faults

    Anything outside those emergency conditions is booked for repair within 3 business days or at the next turnover.

  • Payment timing

    You don't fund a reserve or pay an advance deposit to us. Your maintenance invoices are payable within 7 days.

  • Access preparation

    Before go-live, send us your property access details and any existing cleaning or maintenance contacts so we can coordinate entry.

  • Technical preparation

    Also before go-live, send us the locations of the water, gas and electrical shutoffs and your appliance warranty details, so partners can start without delay.

  • Damage claims

    With Airbnb Booking Management, we file platform claims within 14 days of checkout using walkthrough photos and invoices, and we submit 1 appeal if a claim is denied. We also provide the claim file for your insurer, but we don't cover denied amounts.

  • Refundable deposits

    Under that booking service, wherever a platform supports deposits, we set them up, then claim against them or release them after the walkthrough. Deposits are left out of fee-based revenue.

Cosmetic Upkeep, Supplies and Safety Compliance

  • Cosmetic upkeep

    We arrange cosmetic touch-ups and furniture care through local partners to keep your property guest-ready.

  • Local safety requirements

    We coordinate upkeep to meet your state and local short-term rental safety requirements, including working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and charged fire extinguishers.

  • Emergency information

    As part of your property's routine safety upkeep, we arrange the required posted emergency exits and contact information.

  • Barriers and occupancy

    Partners check the required pool and hot tub barriers and the maximum occupancy shown on your permit.

  • Required inspections

    If your city requires permit or renewal inspections, we book them, arrange corrections for any failed items and keep the records in your permit file.

  • Cleaning supplies

    For Vacation Rental Cleaning, keep 3 sets of linens and towels per bed and bathroom when laundry is done off-site, or 2 when it's washed on-site, plus roughly 4 weeks of amenities.

  • Licensing and taxes

    With Short-Term Rental Licensing & Compliance, your permit comes before go-live. You or your accountant pay any taxes the platforms don't remit, using the monthly documentation we include.

Vetted Local Maintenance Partners and Quality Standards

  • Vendor contracting

    We contract cleaning and maintenance vendors before your first booking, so service is in place from day one.

  • Vendor briefing

    We also brief those vendors before your first booking while we get your property's cleaning and maintenance service ready.

  • Ongoing quality checks

    After go-live, we keep checking the quality of the work your cleaning and maintenance vendors deliver.

  • Central specialist support

    Our remote management team runs the listing performance systems, pricing and guest support, and also coordinates local property services.

  • Local service coverage

    Through Short Term Rental Management, we coordinate your existing team where we don't have coverage, or we find and vet a new local company within 14 days.

  • Insurance requirements

    Our insurance requirements include an active rental policy covering paying guests, with a copy to us before go-live. We also verify partners' insurance and required trade licenses before their first job.

  • Service remedies

    Under our terms, partners redo substandard work for free or are replaced, and we fix our own errors within 2 business days. If we don't meet your expectations, you can cancel with no fee on 30 days' notice, and we hand over within 5 business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I hire you just to coordinate maintenance, or does it come only with full management?

Maintenance coordination is only available as part of our full management service. We don't offer it separately. Full management also takes care of your listing, pricing, guest support and cleaning, and local partners we arrange do all the on-site work. If you choose our reservations-only option, you handle cleaning and maintenance yourself.

If a repair takes several days, how do you fit it around my bookings, and will you block dates on the calendar?

We first try to fit multi-day repairs into open gaps between stays, and we block extra dates only once you approve. When a job needs more nights than a gap allows, we propose dates for your approval before we block your master calendar. If the repair can wait, we send that proposal at least 7 days ahead. We never block dates that overlap a confirmed booking. The dates reopen the same day the partner confirms the work is finished. When parts delay the fix, a local partner we arrange makes a temporary repair. That could mean isolating and drying out a leak, bringing in portable AC or fans, or fitting a temporary lock. We order the parts on the supplier's fastest delivery and chase any delay, keeping you informed.

What will drive my maintenance costs up or down with you, beyond the management fee?

Your costs go up with a larger or older property, with a pool or hot tub that needs weekly service, and with more frequent preventive checks (monthly instead of quarterly). Emergency callouts outside normal hours are billed at partners' after-hours rates, and partners in remote markets charge for their travel time. A newer home, fewer high-upkeep amenities and less frequent checks bring your costs down. Local partners bill you directly at their own rates, and we add no markup. Our 10% management fee stays the same no matter how much maintenance your property needs.

If the AC fails or there's a leak mid-stay, how do you handle refunds or relocating the guest, and do you check with me first?

We approve goodwill refunds of up to USD 200 per reservation without asking you, and the amount is deducted from that booking's payout. A larger refund needs your written approval first. Moving the guest to another nearby stay at your cost also needs your written approval first.

How do you arrange authorized contractor entry while guests are staying and prevent continued access after the job?

We arrange the visit with your guest and give the contractor temporary access for that job only, not ongoing access to your property. When the work is done, we remove that access. We also keep an entry record so you can see who went in and when.

How contractor access is granted, removed and logged when guests are staying.

  1. Agree visit time with guest

    At least 24 hours ahead, or as soon as possible in an emergency.

  2. Issue time-limited access

    A unique entry code that works only for the booked window, or a key through our key-handover partner.

  3. Remove access after job

    The code is deleted within 1 hour, or our partner collects the key the same day.

  4. Log every entry

    Each entry is recorded with its time and contractor, so you can see who was inside your property.
