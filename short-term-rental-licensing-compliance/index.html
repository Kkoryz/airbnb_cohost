Short-Term Rental Compliance Is the Groundwork for Every Listing

Whether we can manage your home as a short-term rental comes down to your local zoning and ordinances.

That's why our short-term rental compliance service brings together two dedicated teams for you: research and compliance.

Whether you already own a home or are thinking about buying one, our researchers study the rules that govern Airbnb and other short-term rentals and point you to the resources you need to check permitted use and the regulations that apply.

A Written Eligibility Summary Before You Make an Offer

Before you make an offer, we give you a written eligibility summary for that specific address. That way, your purchase decision rests on the requirements that apply to that property, not on the city's general rules.

From your enquiry to a written eligibility summary before you make an offer.

  1. Send the property address

    Send us the exact address you are thinking of buying, rather than relying on the city's general rules.

  2. We reply to your enquiry

    We reply within one business day.

  3. Receive your eligibility summary

    Within 3 business days of getting the address: zoning and ordinance status, permit, registration or licensed-manager requirements, night caps or minimum stays, occupancy taxes, and links to the official sources.

  4. Make your offer

    Decide on your offer with the address-specific summary in hand.

Our Research Team Settles Unclear or Conflicting Local Rules

When zoning and licensing rules are unclear or conflict, we ask the local planning or licensing office for a written answer before we advise you to go ahead, and we add its response to your eligibility summary.

Until the point is clarified, we treat the requirement as unresolved. If no clear answer comes back within 10 business days, we mark it unresolved and don't recommend proceeding on it. We don't substitute our own interpretation for the authority's answer.

HOA, Deed and Lease Restrictions Reviewed Before You List

Early in due diligence, we ask you to send your HOA or condo bylaws, CC&Rs, the deed and, if the property is rented, the lease.

With those documents in hand, we review private restrictions alongside city rules, including bans on short-term stays, minimum lease terms and rental caps. Your summary flags any point that needs written HOA or landlord approval before you list.

Your Options When Permits Are Capped or Limited

If your city allows short-term rentals only in owner-occupied homes, or caps the number of permits, we look at stays of 30 nights or more on the same platforms.

Most short-term rental rules cover stays under 30 days, and we confirm the requirements for your address before you commit. You have other options too. You can host while you live in the home as your primary residence.

You can join the city's permit waitlist where there is one. If you're still buying, you can compare nearby areas where permits are available.

The Compliance Team Keeps Your Licenses, Permits and Registrations Current

Expiry alerts

Every permit, license and registration expiry date goes into our compliance calendar. We email you 90 days before each deadline, then send reminders at 60 and 30 days that list any missing documents, inspections or fees.

Renewal filing

We prepare and file your renewal forms, help you submit them at least 30 days before expiry, and follow the application until the renewed permit is issued.

Documents and access

You send us your current permit, proof of short-term rental insurance, any ownership or ID documents we ask for, and access to the licensing portal, or a delegate login where the portal offers one.

Signatures and fees

Where an agent is allowed to file, you give us signed authorization. You sign any documents the permit holder must sign personally, attend owner interviews, and pay license and permit fees directly to the authority.

Lapsed or rejected applications

We take over the renewal or reapplication, ask the issuing office why it refused or what reinstatement requires, prepare a corrected application and follow it through to approval.

Open violations

We help you resolve the cited problems, request any required re-inspections and draft your written response. Our local partners carry out the required inspections, repairs and corrections at the property.

Recovery timing and bookings

Within 1 business day, we tell you what the authority needs. Our target is to resubmit within 10 business days of receiving your documents, and we block new bookings for any dates not covered by a license.

Repair approval limits

When an inspection item fails, we coordinate repairs and re-inspection through vetted local partners. We can approve up to USD 300 per job without asking you, and anything above that needs your written approval.

Staying Informed and Ready When Local Rules Change

  1. Check local changes

    At least once a month, our compliance team checks ordinances, council agendas and licensing notices in every jurisdiction where we manage your property.

  2. Notify you directly

    When a change affects you, we email you its details and effective date within 5 business days. You don't need to forward us a notice first.

  3. Assign required actions

    Within 10 business days, or sooner if the effective date is closer, we send you a written plan for your property. It sets out the required changes, the authority's deadlines and who handles each one: you, our team or a local partner.

  4. Track each completion

    We update the action plan as each item is done and keep it in your property's compliance record.

  5. Identify affected stays

    Within 5 business days of the effective date being announced, we list every confirmed booking that the new rule makes noncompliant.

  6. Carry out your decisions

    You decide on changes, moves, cancellations and refunds. We then adjust guest counts or dates, offer guests alternative dates or cancel through the platform's resolution process. We handle the platform communications and keep guests informed.

Ordinance rules built into daily operations and reporting

Listing and guest rules

We set the platform guest limit to your occupancy cap, and we add quiet hours, parking limits and no-party rules to your house rules.

Screening and messages

We screen every inquiry and confirm the operating rules with each guest in the booking and pre-arrival messages.

Turnover safety checks

At every turnover, our local partners check the smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, fire extinguishers and posted notices, and report anything missing.

Noise response

Where noise limits apply, we offer monitoring that alerts our team, and we contact the guest within 15 minutes of an alert.

Operating records and reports

For every stay, we keep rental-night and guest-count records taken from the platforms and prepare the reports your city or county requires. Where managers are authorized to file, we file them.

Where they aren't, we send them to you to sign at least 7 days before the deadline.

Submitted report copies

Every report we submit goes into your property's compliance record, and if you ask for copies, we send them within 5 business days.

Your Permit Stays in Your Name

  • Your permit ownership

    Your short-term rental permit or registration stays in your name, and we help you apply for it or renew it.

  • Required listing numbers

    Wherever your local rules require it, we show your permit or registration number on every listing.

  • Before going live

    Your listing doesn't go live until the necessary permit or registration has been issued.

  • Tax filing responsibility

    On Booking.com, direct bookings or any other channel that doesn't collect and remit occupancy, lodging or sales tax, you or your accountant file and pay the authorities directly.

  • Monthly tax documentation

    We prepare the monthly tax documentation and records for those returns as part of our management fee, at no extra charge.

  • Insurance and trade licenses

    Send us your paying-guest short-term rental insurance policy before go-live and keep it in place the whole time. Before local cleaning and maintenance partners take their first job, we verify their insurance and any trade licenses they're required to hold.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do i need a license for airbnb?

Whether you need a short-term rental permit, license, registration or occupancy tax account depends on your exact address. Many US cities and counties require them, and some areas require none. We check what applies to you and help you get the necessary permissions in place before go-live.

Can I use your ordinance research or compliance help before, or without, signing a management agreement, and is there a charge for it?

You can get our eligibility research for any address in the United States or Canada before you sign a management agreement, or without signing one at all, with no charge or obligation. Ongoing compliance support comes with a management agreement: applications, renewals and tax documentation are part of the management fee at no extra charge. You pay permit, registration and tax amounts set by the authority directly.

Does your assessment include a legal opinion, or would I need separate counsel before relying on it for a property purchase?

Our assessment is compliance research, not a legal opinion, and we are not a law firm. Before you rely on it for a purchase, you need a local real estate attorney to review the zoning, HOA, deed and title points. We send you and your attorney the written summary, the supporting ordinance sections and our correspondence with the authority.

I already hold permits from my previous manager. Can you take them over, check they're valid and sort out any that have lapsed?

Yes. During onboarding, we check your existing licenses, permits and registrations against the authority's records, including their status, expiry and the name on file, and we check whether a licensed manager is required. Where it's necessary, we help you renew lapsed permissions or reapply in your name. Your listing stays unpublished until every required permission is in place.

Who bears penalties or other losses if a renewal deadline is missed because your team fails to complete an agreed task?

If we miss a task that your management agreement assigns to us, we pay the resulting late fees or penalties and file the catch-up renewal at no extra charge. If a signature, document or payment assigned to you is late, those costs stay with you. When you report an error in our work, we fix it within 2 business days. This commitment covers late fees and penalties, not other losses.

Can you help respond to a violation notice, fine or license suspension, and where does your support stop and legal representation become necessary?

Yes. Forward us the notice, and within 2 business days we review it against your listing and booking records, gather evidence, draft a response or corrective plan for you to sign and arrange the local fixes. Our support covers the paperwork, evidence and coordination. Hearings, appeals, disputed penalties and suspension proceedings require a local attorney, and we brief them with our records.

Will I have access to a property-level record of submitted applications, issued permits, expiration dates and outstanding compliance actions?

Yes. You can see your property's compliance record at any time, with submitted applications, issued permits, registration numbers, expiration dates, outstanding actions and copies of documents. We also send you an updated copy with your monthly statement.
