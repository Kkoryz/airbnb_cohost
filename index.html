Ownership or authorization

Experienced owner support

Clear Fees With No Hidden Markups

  • Consistent fee structure

    Your full-management percentage is the same whatever your property or local market, and you pay no setup, onboarding or hidden charges.

  • Defined revenue base

    The management percentage applies to your nightly-rate and extra-guest revenue after platform host fees. Cleaning fees, taxes and security deposits are left out.

  • Refunds and cancellations

    Refunded amounts are left out, and cancellation payouts you keep under your policy are included. We work out the fee for each stay in the month of checkout.

  • Included management work

    Your management fee covers multi-platform marketing, cleaning coordination, guest care, local-partner support and listing compliance. When you compare managers, look at what's included and the revenue base, not just the headline percentage.

  • Unmarked vendor bills

    Local partners and other third-party vendors bill you directly. When we arrange their work, we add no markup on maintenance or vendor costs.

  • Software and platform charges

    Your full-management software fee covers pricing tools, market data and calendar software, so you buy no separate licenses. Platforms take their host and guest-payment-processing fees before payout, and those fees are not part of our invoice.

  • No long-term commitment

    Our agreement arrangements run month to month with no long-term lock-in, and you can cancel with 30 days’ notice.

  • Service remedy terms

    Under our service terms, partner work that falls short is redone free or reassigned. We fix our own work within 2 business days. If you cancel, we complete the handover within 5 business days.

Your Home, Cared For by Local Partners

  • Coordinated local work

    We arrange independent local partner companies for cleaning, key handover and maintenance. None of our own staff work at your property.

  • Your existing team

    If your on-site team is reliable and available for same-day turnovers, we keep them on. If not, we arrange a vetted local partner.

  • Vacation Rental Cleaning

    Through our Vacation Rental Cleaning service, we coordinate hotel-standard cleaning and sanitizing before and after stays, along with fresh linen, restocking and inspections between guests.

  • Airbnb Property Maintenance

When There's No Partner Nearby

If there's no vetted partner near you, we coordinate your existing on-site team or find and vet a local company within 14 days.

Every turnover is documented, and we keep a backup partner ready in each area. If a turnover is missed, we send another partner the same day and give you the records you need to claim losses from the company responsible.

Partners are independent businesses, and they are liable for their own work, including any damage they cause.

Costs Outside Our Fees

You pay for cleaning, inspections, repairs, restocking and other local jobs at your partners’ rates, within 7 days of each invoice.

If you set your guest cleaning fee high enough to cover turnover invoices, that fee pays for the expense, and it reaches you with each booking. Short-term rental insurance, restocking and taxes need their own place in your budget.

Permit, registration and tax payments go straight to the authorities, and you also pay for any optional add-ons you order, such as photography.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does short-term rental management cost?

Full management costs 10% of rental revenue plus USD 100 per listing per month for software, and there's no setup or onboarding fee. Rental revenue means your nightly-rate and extra-guest income after platform host fees, not counting cleaning fees, taxes and security deposits. Partners charge you separately for cleaning and repairs, and we add no markup. Your statement arrives by the 10th of the next month, and you pay within 7 days by ACH or card. Our à la carte services are listing creation at USD 350 per listing, paid once before work starts, plus dynamic pricing at USD 75 per listing per month and master calendar management at USD 50 per listing per month. Both are billed monthly in advance.

What is short term rental management?

Short-term rental management means we run your rental listing, so you earn from your property without handling the day-to-day hosting. We work remotely inside your own platform accounts, and local partners handle the work at the property.

What does an airbnb property manager do?

We run your listing, pricing, calendar, guest messages and reviews inside your own platform accounts. You give us co-host or team access, so you never share your passwords. For cleaning, key handover and maintenance, we coordinate independent local companies. We have no staff of our own on site.

What exactly do you take off my hands: guest messaging, cleaning, pricing, maintenance, or all of it?

Full management covers all of it. We handle messaging and pricing remotely and coordinate local partners for cleaning and maintenance. Partners invoice you separately for their work. With reservations-only services, property operations stay with you.

Do you manage properties like mine, such as a single apartment or a holiday cabin, in my area?

We assess properties worldwide, mainly in the United States and Canada, from studios to holiday cabins and luxury villas. You need to own the property or be authorized to manage it, and local rules have to allow short-term rentals. We check permit and licensed-manager requirements and look at comparable listings' rates, occupancy and seasonality over the past 12 months. We don't accept properties in places where short-term rentals are prohibited.

Do I need a minimum number of properties or a certain booking history before you'll take me on?

We consider individual properties and new launches as well as established listings. Whether we accept yours depends on your ownership or authorization, the local short-term rental rules and our review of its revenue potential against comparable local listings over the past 12 months.

Can I still block dates to use my place myself while you manage it?

Yes. Availability stays in your control, and you can book personal stays through the owner reservation feature as long as your dates don't overlap bookings already in place.
