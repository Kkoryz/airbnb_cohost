Free Guides, Templates & Checklists

You can use five free resources to prepare and run your short-term rental.

The rules guide helps you check whether your property can be rented and which permits and taxes apply. The listing launch checklist covers photos, descriptions, amenities, minimum stays and calendar rules.

The co-host access guide shows you how to add a team member on Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com without sharing your password. The guest messaging pack gives you 12 ready-to-edit messages, from booking confirmation through checkout.

The turnover checklist covers cleaning, linens, restocking and the damage check for your cleaning and maintenance partners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these resources free, or do I have to be a client to get them?

Every resource is free, and you don't need to be a client. Any short-term rental owner, wherever the property is, can request them, whether you host on your own or work with another manager. You aren't obliged to sign up for our management or co-hosting services.

Do I have to give you my email address to get them?

Yes, because we send every resource by email. Request the ones you need through our enquiry form, and we email them within one business day. We use your address only to send the files you asked for, never sell or share it, and delete it within 2 business days of you asking us to.

How often do you update the guides when local rules or platform policies change?

We review every guide at least once every 3 months. When Airbnb, Vrbo or Booking.com makes a host-policy change that a guide covers, we update the affected guides within 10 business days. Before you act, confirm your local requirements through the official sources linked in the rules guide. Local ordinances differ from city to city and county to county, and platform policies change, so general guidance doesn't replace the current official wording for where you are.

Can someone on your team walk me through a checklist if I get stuck?

Yes. Email us the checklist and the exact step you're stuck on. We'll reply within one business day with written guidance, or, if it's easier for you, set up a 20-minute screen-share call within 3 business days. If you're already a client, the team running your listing walks you through any checklist as part of your engagement.
