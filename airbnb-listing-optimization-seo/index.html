Our Listing Optimization Service, Built on Real Search Data

We pick your listing’s search terms from real search data rather than guesswork.

For your listing optimization, we draw on platform search suggestions and the filters guests use, your listing’s impressions and click-through figures, and the titles, amenities and wording of the 10 closest comparable listings in your market.

We go through these sources during your audit and again every 90 days.

Listing Content & Photo Optimization That Follows Airbnb's Rules

  • Consistent property strengths

    We build your title, description and captions around the photos and spaces that set your property apart, so every part of the listing describes the same strengths.

  • Photo improvements

    We reorder your existing photos, adjust only light, contrast and color, caption every image and point out any missing shots. An optional professional shoot fills those gaps and shows your rooms accurately.

  • Publishing checks

    We check your rewritten content and photos against Airbnb’s policies. Titles stay within 50 characters, and we leave out contact details, links, off-platform offers, discriminatory language and features the property doesn’t have. Any required permit numbers appear before launch.

  • Availability checks

    When your listing disappears from search, we look at blocked or unavailable dates, minimum-stay and trip-length restrictions, and whether the listing is snoozed or unpublished.

  • Other search blockers

    We also look for missing permit details, incomplete amenity fields and rates set far above comparable listings, so we can pin down what’s keeping your listing from being seen.

  • Search recovery

    We fix the issues we find within 5 business days, then track your position on fixed test searches every week until your listing shows up again.

  • Experienced remote team

    Our remote team manages over 110 properties and has more than 10 years of hosting experience. On a discovery call, we show you anonymized comparable results for the 12 months before and the 12 months after takeover, or since go-live if that’s shorter.

  • Ongoing guest care

    With Airbnb Co-Hosting, we check in with guests during their stay, ask for honest reviews within 24 hours of checkout, send 1 reminder 3 days later if there’s no answer, and reply within 48 hours. We treat every guest equally and offer no incentives.

How Soon Your Listing Rankings Typically Improve

Most listings show measurable ranking gains within 2–4 weeks of optimization: in 2025, 9 of the 14 Airbnb listings we optimized did, measured as each listing's average position across a fixed set of guest searches, recorded weekly.

How quickly yours moves depends on the platform, competing listings, your open dates and booking activity, so we judge results over several weeks of bookings, not the first few days after the changes go live.

How We Track and Prove Your Ranking Gains

  1. Define repeatable searches

    Before we edit anything, we set up searches for your area using three date ranges that stay the same each time, one guest count and two filter combinations.

  2. Capture your baseline

    Before we change your listing, we record its starting search position, listing views, click-through rate and conversion rate.

  3. Repeat weekly checks

    Every week, we run those searches while signed out, log each position and compare the results with the impressions and click-through figures in your Airbnb insights.

  4. Verify genuine gains

    We count your ranking as improved only when your average position rises across the whole set of searches, never because of a single result.

  5. Report and recommend

    Thirty days after optimization, we send you a before-and-after comparison based on your insights, along with recommended changes. From then on, your monthly reports include these metrics alongside occupancy and revenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to optimize airbnb listing?

We optimize your listing by matching its content to what guests search for and by coordinating your photos, pricing, booking settings, responses and calendar availability.

Can I hire you just to optimize my Airbnb listing once, or is optimization only available as part of an ongoing management plan?

Yes, you can book a standalone project without signing up for full management or reservations-only co-hosting. We audit and rebuild your title, description, photos and their order, amenity fields and listing settings, and we change nothing outside that scope. If you have a co-host or revenue manager, they carry on with their work. Before we start, we agree in writing who owns which settings, and we share our change log with you. You keep your listing, your access and your control, and you can add ongoing services later.

Do you optimize my Vrbo and Booking.com listings for their search too, or only my Airbnb listing?

Yes, we optimize your listings on Vrbo, Booking.com and the other platforms we manage, and we adapt to each site's search requirements. On Vrbo that covers the property-type and amenity fields, and on Booking.com the room and facility settings.

Does this service address only Airbnb's internal search, or do you also work on visibility in Google and other external search engines?

We cover search inside Airbnb and the other platforms we manage. We also offer work on Google, AI answer engines, generative AI tools and a direct booking presence. The external work includes Google Vacation Rentals listings and keeping your property names, descriptions and photos consistent so search engines match your listings.

What record of your work will I receive, such as audit findings, final listing copy, a photo plan or a summary of settings changed?

Within 5 business days of publication, you get the audit findings, the final titles and descriptions for each platform, a photo shot list and photo order, and every booking or listing setting we changed with its old and new values. Any later updates go into your monthly performance report.

Can you optimize a listing connected to my existing property management system or channel manager without synced content overwriting your changes?

Yes. We check which fields your system pushes to each platform, and we edit synced titles, descriptions, photos and amenities inside that system. When an edit can only be made on the platform, we turn off syncing for that field with your approval. We recheck the live listing 24 hours after each change.

If visibility or bookings do not improve after the expected initial period, what diagnostic and revision work is included?

If your ranking or bookings haven't improved after 4 weeks, we review impressions, click-through, booking rate, pricing and competing listings. Within 5 business days, we revise your titles, photos, amenities or settings in phases, and this is part of your engagement. We repeat these reviews every 30 days until a review finds nothing left to change, and most listings get there within 30 to 90 days, as 9 of the 14 Airbnb listings we optimized in 2025 did.
