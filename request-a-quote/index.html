What to Share About Your Property and Its Results

When you ask for a rental property management quote, send us the city and state, the property type, how many bedrooms and bathrooms it has and how many guests it sleeps.

If it's already listed, add your Airbnb, Vrbo or Booking.com listing links and roughly the last 12 months of occupancy and nightly rates.

Let us know whether you own the property or are authorized to manage it, whether your area requires a short-term rental permit or registration and whether you hold one, and whether you have a cleaning or maintenance team we'd work with.

Your Free Earnings Estimate and Revenue Projection

  • Free analysis and plan

    We put together an earnings estimate and a plan from your property details at no charge and with no obligation. Then we walk you through the projection on a free discovery call.

  • Details improve the match

    Send us your amenities, photos and any past booking history. With complete details, we match your home to genuinely comparable local listings. When details are missing, we have to use broader, less representative matches.

  • Local evidence, not guarantees

    Your projection is built on the nightly rates, occupancy and seasonality of comparable local listings over the past 12 months. It's an estimate, not a guarantee.

  • Gross revenue and net payout

    You see gross rental revenue next to your estimated net payout after platform host fees, our management fee and the monthly software fee per listing. Cleaning fees, taxes and security deposits are excluded.

The First Terms You'll See Before Signing

Your proposal states a single management fee: 10% of rental revenue after platform host fees, with cleaning fees, taxes and security deposits left out.

On top of it comes a USD 100 monthly software fee per listing. Your management agreement carries the same terms unchanged, and the rate stays the same whatever your property's size, booking volume or number of platforms.

Clear Fees, No Lock-In and Secure Platform Access

  • Look beyond the rate

    What you take home depends on the revenue our management brings in, so the fee rate on its own doesn't tell you what you'll earn.

  • No long-term lock-in

    Our month-to-month agreements don't tie you in for the long term, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

  • Secure platform access

    With platform team access, you keep your passwords private. Once the engagement ends, we remove our access within 1 business day and delete any copies of your data we hold within 30 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the proposal free, and am I committed to anything once I receive it?

Yes, your proposal and revenue analysis are free, and you're under no obligation to go ahead. Send us your property details and we'll prepare an earnings estimate and plan, then walk you through the projection on a free 30-minute discovery call.

What if my property is new and has no bookings or revenue yet?

Your property doesn't need its own booking history. We use the nightly rates, occupancy and seasonality of comparable local listings over the past 12 months. The projection is an estimate, not a guarantee. It shows your gross rental revenue and your estimated net payout after platform host fees, our 10% fee and the monthly software fee per listing. Cleaning fees, taxes and security deposits aren't included. If you're turning a home into a short-term rental, we help you choose the property, furnish it and set it up, and then we manage it from there.

Will you visit the property before you send me a proposal?

No. We work entirely online, and none of our own staff are at any property. We handle your listings, pricing, calendars, guest messages and reviews through co-host or team access in your own platform accounts. You stay the account holder, so your listing, booking history, reviews and payouts remain yours when the engagement ends.

Will you keep the booking and revenue figures I send you confidential?

Yes. We use your account, payout and guest data only to run your listing. We never sell it or share it for marketing, and only the team members working on your account can access it. Guest personal details from platform bookings stay inside the booking platforms, and we delete any copies we hold within 30 days of the engagement ending.
