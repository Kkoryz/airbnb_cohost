When We Decline a Booking & Why

We only turn down a booking for reasons tied to the stay itself: the guest hasn't completed the platform's ID verification, host reviews report damage or broken house rules, there are signs of a party or event, or the group is larger than your listing's capacity.

Our Airbnb booking management never turns a guest away because of race, religion, national origin, gender, disability or any other characteristic protected by the platform's non-discrimination policy.

24/7 Guest Messaging and Inquiry Screening

We answer inquiries and booking requests 24/7, within minutes.

Request screening

Before we accept a request, we check that the guest has platform-verified ID and no negative host reviews, that the group is within your occupancy limits, and that they have confirmed they agree to your house rules, including no parties.

Instant Book checks

Guests must be platform-verified and have good reviews. We review every instant booking within 1 hour and confirm the trip purpose and group size. If a red flag isn't resolved, we request a penalty-free cancellation where platform rules allow it.

Scheduled routine messages

Booking confirmations, check-in instructions with addresses and entry codes, checkout reminders and review requests go out as scheduled templates from your own platform accounts.

Your earnings estimate

Send us your property details for a free, no-obligation revenue analysis and plan. We then explain your revenue projection on a free 30-minute discovery call.

Personal replies

A team member answers special requests, complaints, late checkout requests and any question your guidebook doesn't cover. Any exception to your agreed rules, including discounts or extra guests, reaches you within 1 hour.

Reporting and alerts

You get an alert for every new booking and a monthly report on response rate, response time, occupancy, nightly rates and booking counts. You also get a statement showing total revenue, our fee, expenses and your net payout.

Our company

Our remote management and co-hosting company runs short-term rental listings for owners and holds Airbnb Superhost and Vrbo Premier Host status.

Related Services for Airbnb Hosts

  • Guest data privacy

    Under our privacy policy, guest identity verification for platform bookings stays inside the booking platforms and account access is limited to your team. Any copies we hold are deleted within 30 days of the engagement ending.

  • Cleaning and damage

    With Vacation Rental Cleaning, our partners check for damage and missing items when they arrive. We tell you straight away and file platform claims within 14 days of checkout.

  • Property maintenance

    Through our Airbnb Property Maintenance service, we work with local vendors to coordinate routine upkeep and on-call repairs, including pools, hot tubs, lawns and outdoor areas.

Calendar Management and Multi-Platform Sync

  • One master calendar

    One master calendar is the source of truth for all your booking channels, and we check its sync status every hour.

  • Connection failures

    If sync drops or lags, we close the affected dates by hand on every other platform within 1 hour. We reopen them only after sync is confirmed.

  • Direct and returning bookings

    We add direct and returning-guest reservations next to your Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com bookings. Those dates are blocked on every other channel within 1 hour of confirmation.

  • Returning guest enquiries

    Forward us the dates your returning guest wants. We confirm the stay, send the check-in details and handle their messages just as we do for platform reservations.

  • Co-hosting connections

    Airbnb Co-Hosting extends your listings to Vrbo, Booking.com, Google Vacation Rentals, Expedia and direct channels. You can keep your existing tools or connect our channel manager, and your confirmed bookings aren't interrupted either way.

How We Take Over Your Booking Management

  1. Audit your setup

    Before we take over guest communication, we review your current listing, the way you message guests and how your calendar is set up.

  2. Agree your strategy

    Together we agree on pricing, booking preferences, minimum stays and the guest profiles you want to attract, all within platform non-discrimination rules.

  3. Choose booking control

    If you want to approve bookings yourself, we turn Instant Book off on the platforms you pick. Or we leave it on and build your agreed criteria into the guest requirements.

  4. Hand over communication

    We take over inquiries, reservations and guest messages completely only after we've agreed on the audit and the strategy.

  5. Review screened requests

    When Instant Book is off, we send you every booking request with our screening notes within 1 hour, and you make the call.

  6. Prevent expired requests

    Airbnb gives you 24 hours to accept a request. If you haven't replied within 12 hours, we decide based on your agreed booking criteria. That keeps the request from expiring and protects your response rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

For channels or rate plans where the property has to collect payment, do you manage payment requests, failed payments and security deposits, or do I handle them?

We manage payment requests and follow up on any that fail. If a reservation stays unpaid, we need your approval to cancel it under your policy. Payouts go straight into your own platform accounts, and we never hold your rental income. On platforms that support it, including Vrbo, we set a refundable security deposit. After the post-stay walkthrough, we either claim against it or release it. Deposits don't count toward the revenue we use to calculate our fee.

If a double booking still happens, how do you fix it, and who pays any rebooking cost or platform penalty?

We move the affected guest to a comparable stay nearby on the same day. We also handle the cancellation through the platform so it's recorded correctly. If our calendar handling caused the conflict, we pay the platform penalties and any extra rehousing costs. If the conflict came from a stay you booked or blocked outside the master calendar, you pay those costs.

Can you manage linked calendars if I rent out individual rooms and the whole property as separate listings?

Yes. We link your room listings and your whole-property listing in the master calendar. A whole-home booking blocks every room, and any room booking blocks the whole home across Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and your other platforms. During onboarding we test every channel with a trial block, and we test again whenever you add or change a listing.

If an urgent guest problem needs someone on site and the local partner isn't available, what do I or my local contact have to do?

Under full management, you don't have to go to the property: we arrange backup help on site and call you to agree on any refund for nights the guest couldn't use. With booking management only, on-site help stays with you and your local contacts, and we keep the guest updated. Before go-live, you also give us property access details, your existing cleaning or maintenance contacts, and the locations of the water, gas and electrical shutoffs.

Can I set spending limits so you need my approval before offering refunds, discounts or other guest compensation?

Yes. By default, we can offer up to USD 200 per booking in goodwill refunds, discounts and other compensation, and that amount comes out of the booking's payout. Anything larger needs your written approval. You can lower this limit all the way to zero. We record it during onboarding and ask for your approval before offering anything above it. If your limit covers routine complaints, we settle those complaints on the spot. Refunds required by your listing's cancellation policy or by a platform decision go through without prior approval.

When a guest wants to cancel, change dates or get a refund, who decides, and what cancellation policy will you set on my listings?

We accept date changes that fall within your pricing limits, unless you want to approve every change yourself. We process cancellations and refunds that follow your listing's policy or a platform decision. During onboarding, we walk you through the policy options and recommend terms that suit your market and ranking: Firm on Airbnb, Moderate on Vrbo, and on Booking.com a flexible rate with free cancellation up to 14 days plus a non-refundable rate. The final choice is yours, and you can make any listing stricter or more flexible whenever you like.
