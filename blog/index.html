  • What Is the 80/20 Rule for Airbnb?

    17 November 2026

    The 80/20 rule for Airbnb is the Pareto principle applied to hosting: as Lodgify puts it, roughly 80% of your results come from 20% of your efforts. Use it to decide where to focus, not to expect that exact revenue split.

  • What Should My Co-Hosting or Vacation Rental Management Agreement Include?

    17 November 2026

    Your vacation rental management agreement should identify the parties and property, included services, exclusions, owner and vendor duties, decision rights, fees, payouts, reporting, compliance responsibilities, insurance, account access, and exit handover. Attach schedules that turn those headings into named people, defined costs, and observable results.

    Read more →

  • What Is a Reasonable Fee for Managing or Co-Hosting My Vacation Rental?

    17 November 2026

    A reasonable vacation rental management fee buys clearly defined work, leaves you an acceptable payout and removes responsibilities you want to delegate. Published full-service estimates span roughly 15–35%, while partial-service examples start at 10–15%; scope and the revenue being charged matter more than the headline rate. Houfy, Triad Vacation Rentals, Evolve.

    Read more →

  • How Can I Calculate and Track My Vacation Rental Profitability?

    17 November 2026

    Calculate vacation rental profitability by subtracting operating expenses from rental revenue, then account separately for debt payments, capital purchases and reserves. Track those figures monthly for each property so a strong booking calendar does not hide a cash shortage.
