Airbnb Co-Hosting Without Giving Up Your Listing

Your listing stays yours, and so does control over it.

Through our Airbnb co-hosting service, we take on the day-to-day work by connecting to the listing you already have instead of replacing it.

You can block dates for personal stays at any time, either in the owner portal or by telling our team, and we plan bookings around your schedule.

Secure Co-Host Access That Keeps Your Password Private

You add us through each platform’s co-host or team-access feature, so you never share your password.

Ownership, payouts and security settings stay with you, and you can restrict or remove our access at any time. Full management needs full co-host access, because that is how we edit your listing, set rates and calendar rules, and reply to guests.

Reservations-only service needs calendar and inbox access, and that level of access doesn't let us change listing content or pricing rules unless you add dynamic pricing and the access it needs.

Our privacy policy covers how we handle data: we use it only to deliver the service, guest details from platform bookings stay with the platforms, team access is restricted, and nothing is shared for marketing or sold.

When the engagement ends, we remove our access within 1 business day and delete any copies we hold within 30 days.

Clear Management Fees and What the Commission Covers

  • Full management fees

    You pay 10% of rental revenue plus USD 100 per listing per month for software. There are no setup fees or hidden costs.

  • Commission calculation

    Commission is based on nightly-rate and extra-guest revenue after platform host fees. Cleaning fees, taxes, security deposits and refunded amounts are left out.

  • Checkout accounting

    We calculate commission for each stay in the month the guest checks out, not the month the booking was made.

  • Listing creation

    À la carte listing creation is a one-time USD 350 per listing, with no revenue-percentage fee.

  • Dynamic pricing

    À la carte dynamic pricing costs USD 75 per listing per month, with no revenue-percentage fee.

  • Master calendar management

    À la carte master calendar management costs USD 50 per listing per month, with no revenue-percentage fee.

  • Work you retain

    With à la carte services, cleaning coordination and issue handling stay with you. Under full management, we handle all of these as well as pricing.

Revenue Projections, Direct Payouts & Monthly Invoicing

  • Revenue projection

    On your free 30-minute discovery call, we show you gross rental revenue side by side with your estimated net payout after our fees and platform host fees.

  • Direct payouts

    Under our management arrangements, platforms pay into your accounts on their normal schedules. Your monthly statement and invoice arrive by the 10th, and the invoice is due within 7 days by ACH or card.

  • Separate operating costs

    Under our operating-cost arrangements, you cover partner invoices, which are due within 7 days and carry no markup, along with channel fees, insurance, optional add-ons and authority charges. Listing promotion is included.

  • Final management charges

    If you cancel, commission applies only to stays that check out by your end date, plus the software fee for the final month.

We Take On a Limited Number of Owners

Limited onboarding

We take on only a limited number of new co-hosting partners at a time, so our portfolio grows deliberately and not for the sake of volume.

Property acceptance

We only accept properties we can manage properly. That protects our response times and the attention your listing gets.

Onboarding availability

If our onboarding capacity is full, you wait for a slot instead of starting straight away with an overstretched team.

Service standards

Under our service terms, a partner redoes work at no extra cost or is replaced, we make our own fixes within 2 business days, you can cancel on 30 days’ notice with no fees, and we complete the handover within 5 business days.

Remote delivery

Our remote team works with owners worldwide, mainly in the United States and Canada, and has no on-site employees. Where it's needed, we coordinate your existing team or find and vet a local partner within 14 days.

Evidence before signing

You see our host-profile verification and performance evidence before you sign.

We share profile links on request, and on your discovery call we show you an anonymized statement and comparable results covering the 12 months before and the 12 months after takeover.

Licensing and taxes

Short-Term Rental Licensing & Compliance supports the permits you hold as owner before launch, and we prepare your monthly tax records at no extra charge. You or your accountant file and pay any taxes the platforms don't remit.

Insurance requirements

Under our insurance requirements, you arrange and maintain short-term rental coverage for paying guests before launch. We check local partners’ insurance and required trade licenses before their first job.

Co-Host Support for Live Listings, Bookings and New Rentals

  • Existing listing management

    If your listing is already live, we audit, optimize and reposition it first, so its content, pricing and positioning are competitive before ongoing remote management begins.

  • Booking management authority

    Airbnb Booking Management screens reservations and handles changes in line with policy. Goodwill compensation comes out of the booking's payout and is capped at USD 200 per booking unless you set a lower ceiling, and that ceiling can be zero. Anything larger needs your written approval.

  • Conversion and setup

    If you're converting a home into a short-term rental, Airbnb Design & Furnishing supports you all the way from choosing and furnishing the property through setup and into ongoing management.

Search-Ready Listings and a Fast Launch

Once your home is guest-ready, it goes live within 7–14 days.

Photography, listing optimization and compliance paperwork all run at the same time, so you don't wait for one to finish before the next starts. Airbnb Listing Optimization & SEO improves your listing's structure and search-focused descriptions.

Optional photography costs USD 250 per shoot and 3D tours cost USD 150 per property. Both are invoiced on completion and payable within 7 days.

Within 3 business days of receiving your property details, we send separate quotes for furnishing and smart-home setup, and suppliers and installers bill you directly with no markup from us.

More Ways We Support Your Rental

With Dynamic Pricing, we manage your minimum stays, booking-window limits and gap-night rules together rather than one at a time. That keeps longer reservations, occupancy and peak weekend demand in balance and leaves fewer empty nights between bookings.

Turnover Coordination, Checkout Inspections & Damage Claims

  • Checkout inspections

    After every checkout, our local partner checks your home for damage and maintenance needs. We document any problem straight away with dated evidence tied to that specific stay, and we support your Airbnb claims.

  • Unsafe guest behavior

    If a guest's behavior threatens people or your home, we contact the police or emergency services, notify you within 1 hour and document the damage.

  • Turnover cleaning

    Vacation Rental Cleaning coordinates turnovers to a hotel standard. Under full management, you approve a cleaning fee that is competitive and covers costs, and we review it whenever partner rates change or every 6 months.

  • Maintenance response

    Airbnb Property Maintenance gives you 24/7 emergency coordination. We activate our response immediately, dispatch a local partner quickly and handle guest communication throughout the repair.

  • Repair spending limits

    We authorize up to USD 300 per repair job. Larger repairs need your written approval before work starts.

  • Smaller repair approvals

    You can require approval for smaller jobs too. Tell us in writing before we authorize them.

  • Emergency spending exception

    If we can't reach you and safety or ongoing damage calls for action, we authorize up to USD 1,000 and notify you within 1 hour.

Multi-Platform Distribution Beyond Airbnb

Wider channel distribution

Beyond Airbnb, we list your property on Vrbo, Booking.com, Google Vacation Rentals, Expedia and direct-booking channels. Everything syncs through one master calendar, which prevents double bookings.

Your direct-booking website

We set up and run a property website with secure checkout, or we connect your existing direct-booking website to our master calendar.

Direct-stay operations

We manage rates, availability and guest messages for direct bookings, and guest payments go into a payment account held in your name.

Included software licenses

Under full management, your stated software fee covers our channel manager, pricing and calendar tools, so you don't buy separate licenses.

Direct-booking trade-offs

Direct bookings avoid channel host fees. In exchange, you pay domain and card-processing costs directly, and you give up the guest-vetting and protection programs that booking platforms offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does airbnb co hosting work?

First we complete onboarding and an audit and agree a strategy with you. Then we take over the agreed operations inside your existing listing. Our team handles the remote work, and local partners take care of tasks at the property.

What does an airbnb co host do?

We manage your listing, nightly pricing, calendar, guest messages and reviews from inside your own Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and other platform accounts. We also coordinate local companies for cleaning, key handover and maintenance.

Can you work with my existing property management system, channel manager and automated messaging tools, or must I replace them?

You don't need to replace anything. We work alongside the tools you already use, and if we haven't connected your software before, we set up the connection. To manage rates, calendars and messages, we use a channel-manager team login that you can restrict or revoke. Your templates and automations stay active, and we review and improve them with you. If you don't have a channel manager or want to leave yours, we connect your listings to ours during onboarding without interrupting any confirmed bookings.

Do my existing reviews and host status stay with my listing, and if I end the agreement, is the listing left intact in my account?

Yes. We work inside your accounts, so your listing, reviews, ratings and any Superhost status stay in place. We also keep our response times fast enough to protect your eligibility. When the agreement ends, your listing, reviews and future bookings stay intact in your account, and we remove our access within 1 business day.

If I cancel, what happens to bookings already on the calendar and to the cleaners and vendors you arranged?

Existing bookings stay in your accounts and go ahead as normal. We keep messaging guests and coordinating cleans until your end date. Within 5 business days of that date, we send you a handover file with open bookings, partner contacts, access details and statements. You can keep working with the same cleaners and vendors directly.

My listing already has a co-host, or I handle some guest messages myself. Can you take over from them, or work alongside them?

Yes. We can replace your co-host, or we can share the work with them or with you. We agree a clear split of tasks so there are no gaps and no duplicate replies. For example, we handle pricing and calendars while you answer selected messages. For a complete takeover, you invite us through the platform's co-host feature and remove the previous co-host once we're live. Your existing listing carries on and doesn't need to be rebuilt.
