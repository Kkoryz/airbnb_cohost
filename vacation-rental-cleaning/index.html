Hotel-Standard Cleaning Services Between Every Stay

We schedule and supervise local cleaning partners who leave your rental clean, stocked and at a hotel standard between stays.

Our vacation rental cleaning serves homes with frequent guest changes. We hand-pick these partners, and they're independent companies, not our employees. Before a partner's first job, we check its insurance, its required trade licenses and the background checks for the staff who will attend your property.

Each partner is liable for its own work, including any damage its cleaners cause. We give you the records you need to claim losses from the responsible partner.

Launch Cleans, Deep Cleans and Mid-Stay Refreshes

You choose which additional cleans you want. We schedule them around stays so no booked nights are blocked.

Launch clean

Before your listing goes live and your first guests arrive, our local partners clean your property from top to bottom.

Deep clean

Every 3 months or after each peak season, our partners clean inside the appliances, behind the furniture, and the upholstery and grout.

Mid-stay refresh

On bookings of 7 nights or longer, you can add a clean partway through the stay to refresh the property.

Already Have a Cleaner? We Coordinate Their Turnovers

If you'd rather keep your own cleaner, we coordinate their turnovers through our shared calendar and photo reporting and hold them to the same standards as our partners. With Airbnb Co-Hosting, we optimize and reposition existing live listings before managing them remotely.

We're a short-term rental management and co-hosting company that runs owners' listings remotely.

How your own cleaner works with our turnover calendar and photo reporting.

  1. Join shared turnover calendar

    Your cleaner gives us a phone number and email and accepts access to our shared turnover calendar, which syncs with your master calendar.

  2. Bookings appear automatically

    Every new booking, change or cancellation shows up for your cleaner automatically.

  3. Reminder 24 hours before

    We send a reminder with checkout time and guest count 24 hours before each turnover.

  4. Cleaner completes the turnover

    Your cleaner works to the same standard as our partners.

  5. Photos and flags sent

    Your cleaner sends us photos after each clean and flags any damage or low supplies within 2 hours of finishing.

What Each Guest Turnover Covers

Every turnover covers the whole interior that guests use, plus the outdoor seating areas.

  • Indoor rooms

    Our partners clear out whatever the previous guests left behind and wipe down every surface, including living rooms, dining areas, entryways and game rooms.

  • Patios and balconies

    During the turnover, guest-use areas are swept, outdoor furniture is wiped down and the trash is emptied.

  • Excluded spaces

    Pools, hot tubs, lawns, garages and owner-locked storage aren't part of turnover cleaning. Our maintenance partners look after them instead.

Your Cleaning Checklist, Surface Care and Products

We hand our partners your cleaning checklist, take out any tasks you handle separately and brief them on your surface-care rules, such as pH-neutral cleaner on natural stone or no bleach on specified fixtures.

If you need fragrance-free or other specific products, partners use matching products from their kit. If you keep your preferred brand at the property, they use it at every clean.

Photo Checks Before Every Guest Arrival

  1. Document the clean

    After every turnover, our partner sends timestamped photos of each room and the restocked amenities, along with confirmation that the lock or lockbox works.

  2. Review before arrival

    Our team checks the photos and access confirmation as soon as the partner sends them, before your next guest checks in, so we never rely on a reported finish.

  3. Correct before check-in

    If anything is missing, the partner fixes it before check-in where the window allows, or returns within 6–24 hours at no extra cost; substandard work means a redo or a new partner.

Turnover Pricing, Extra Charges and Billing

  • Property-specific rates

    Your local partner prices your turnovers on floor area, bedroom and bathroom counts, laundry load and the condition guests leave the place in.

  • Additional cleaning charges

    Off-site laundry, pet-hair removal, heavy soiling and deep-clean work at your property are charged as extras.

  • Date and notice charges

    Your cleaning partner also charges extra for holiday dates and for turnovers booked less than 24 hours ahead.

  • Direct partner billing

    The partner invoices you directly at its own rates. We add nothing, and payment is due within 7 days of the invoice date.

  • Coordination included

    Arranging your turnovers is part of our management fee, so cleaning coordination costs you nothing extra.

  • Guest fee offset

    Setting your listing’s cleaning fee against the partner’s actual rate covers most of the cost, and you receive that fee with each booking payout.

Bedroom Linen Changes and Guest Staging

  • Fresh bedroom linen

    Our partners strip the dirty linens and towels, wash them and make up the beds with fresh linen for your next guests.

  • Guest-ready staging

    Our partners restock guest essentials and set up your unit to your written specifications. Reference photos help them match the home shown in your listing.

Cleaning Supplies, Guest Amenities and Laundry

Supplies and amenities

Our partners bring their own professional-grade cleaning supplies and equipment. You provide the guest amenities, including toilet paper and soap, from your property's own stock.

Laundry arrangements

When your on-site machines can keep pace, we use them, and you need 2 complete linen and towel sets for each bed and bathroom. Larger homes and tight turnovers use off-site laundry, or rental where it's available.

Off-site washing needs 3 sets.

Off-site collection

At each turnover, our partner collects the soiled linens and towels and returns them within 24–48 hours. In the meantime, cleaners use the spare sets and spend less time on site.

Local linen rental

Where a local laundry partner offers rental, we arrange for sheets and towels to be supplied, collected and laundered at each turnover. The partner invoices you directly.

Owned linens and furnishing

With Airbnb Design & Furnishing, our supply partner provides move-in kits with kitchen essentials, linens and towels that you own, along with amenities such as kitchenware, board games and pool toys.

Your stock targets

Keep about 4 weeks' worth of guest amenities on hand, including toilet paper, soap and coffee. Tell us your preferred products and targets, such as 2 spare toilet paper rolls per bathroom.

Stock checks and alerts

Partners count supplies as part of every turnover checklist. When an item drops below a 2-week supply, we let you know within 1 business day.

Approved replenishment

Once you approve, we reorder your chosen products through our supply partner to bring stock back to target levels. Every 3 months, we go over the quantities with you and compare them with usage.

Damage and Missing-Item Reports After Checkout

We coordinate reports of damage and missing items after a stay through the claim process. With Airbnb Booking Management, we also arrange refundable security deposits where the platform supports them, including Vrbo. After the walkthrough, we either claim against the deposit or release it.

Deposits are left out of the revenue we use to calculate our fee.

From the post-stay check to the damage claim.

  1. Check on arrival

    Partners check the property for damage and missing items as soon as they arrive, before they start cleaning, so any problem is tied to the stay that just ended.

  2. You are told immediately

    We tell you about any damage or missing items right away.

  3. Claim within 14 days

    We file the claim on Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com or the other platform within 14 days of checkout, using photos and invoices from the post-stay walkthrough.

  4. One appeal if denied

    If the platform denies the claim, we file 1 appeal.

  5. Claim file for insurer

    We do not cover the denied amount ourselves, but we give you the full claim file to use with your insurer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I engage you just to coordinate turnover cleaning while keeping listing management and guest communication with my current team?

Yes. You can hire us just to coordinate turnover cleaning, and your current team keeps handling listing management and guest communication. We take care of all contact with local cleaning partners and pass on the schedules from your booking calendar along with your special requests.

How much should I charge for Airbnb cleaning?

Under full management, we propose a guest cleaning fee that covers the partner's turnover invoice, including laundry and restocking. We compare it with similar listings nearby so the guest's total stays competitive. The fee goes live only after you approve it. We review it whenever the partner's rate changes, and at least every 6 months. Cleaning fees come to you with each booking payout and are excluded from the rental revenue we use to calculate our 10% management fee.

Can you coordinate turnovers through my existing property-management or cleaning app, or would I need to change systems?

No, you don't need to change systems. We connect cleaning assignments to most major property-management platforms through a direct calendar link or your channel manager. If your cleaning app offers team or partner access, we work inside it. If it doesn't, we sync turnovers from your platform calendars.

If a guest reports a missed cleaning task at arrival, how quickly can your partners return to correct it?

The cleaning partner comes back within 6–24 hours to redo the missed area, at no extra cost to you. If that partner can't return within that window, we send a different local partner to make the correction.

Which conditions, such as severe smoke residue, bodily fluids or pest contamination, are outside turnover-cleaning scope and require specialist treatment?

Turnover cleaning doesn't cover severe smoke residue, blood or other bodily fluids beyond normal bathroom use, pest infestations, animal waste beyond routine pet hair, or any repair and restoration work. When our cleaning partners find any of these conditions, they photograph them. We then arrange a separate assessment by a licensed specialist, such as a biohazard, odor-remediation or pest-control company, and the specialist invoices you directly.

How do cleaners distinguish forgotten belongings from trash, and who handles storage and return of items found during a turnover?

Our cleaning partners throw away only items that are clearly disposable, such as food leftovers, empty packaging and used toiletries. Anything that could have value, including clothing, chargers, documents and jewelry, gets photographed, bagged, labeled and kept as a found item. The partner stores it securely for 30 days. We contact the guest through the booking platform within 24 hours, and if they want the item back, we arrange shipping at their cost.

Can you handle laundry for a smaller property with no washer or dryer, or when its machines break down?

Yes. When your property has no washer or dryer, we arrange for a local laundry partner to collect the used linens and bring back clean sets. For this off-site laundry service, you keep 3 full sets of linens and towels for each bed and bathroom. If your machines break down, we move that turnover to off-site laundry the same day and arrange the appliance repair through our maintenance partners.

What checkout-to-check-in window do you require for my property, including days when several of my units turn over at once?

We need at least 4 hours between checkout and check-in for homes with up to 3 bedrooms, and 5 hours for larger homes. A standard 11 a.m. checkout with a 4 p.m. check-in meets both. When several of your units turn over on the same day, each unit gets its own partner crew, so the same window applies to every property.
