Who We Are and How We Work Remotely

We manage your rental listing remotely, inside your own platform accounts, and we don't keep any staff of our own at your property.

Our short-term rental management and co-hosting service looks after your listing, pricing, calendar, guest messages and reviews, and our team does all of it online. For cleaning, key handover and maintenance at your property, we arrange and coordinate local partner companies.

You decide the pet policy, and when you need it, we arrange extra cleaning or maintenance plans to support it.

What Are Your Contract, Insurance and Cancellation Terms?

  • Contract and cancellation

    We work month to month, with no long-term lock-in. You can cancel with 30 days’ notice, and there's no cancellation fee.

  • Your property insurance

    Before go-live, send us your property insurance policy. It has to explicitly cover short-term rental use with paying guests, and you need to keep it active for as long as we work together.

  • Homeowner policy exclusions

    If your homeowner policy excludes paying guests, you're exposed from the very first booking, and that policy doesn't meet our insurance requirement.

  • Insured local partners

    Every cleaning and maintenance partner we arrange has to carry its own insurance. We check that insurance, along with any required trade license, before their first job.

  • Final management fee

    After your end date, we invoice our 10% fee only on stays that have checked out by that date. Stays that check out later carry no fee from us.

  • Final software charge

    Your closing charges also include the monthly software fee per listing for the final month.

  • Final statement payment

    We send your final statement by the 10th of the following month, and payment is due within 7 days by ACH or card.

  • Taxes on services

    Our quoted management fee and service prices don't include taxes. If your state taxes these fees, we add sales tax as a separate line on your monthly invoice.

How Soon Can My Home Go Live and Take Bookings?

Your home goes live within 7–14 days and starts taking bookings once onboarding is complete.

Signing up takes less than 15 minutes. Full optimization usually takes 30 to 90 days, as it did for 9 of the 14 Airbnb listings in our 2025 listing optimization work. That time lets your new listing gain visibility, collect reviews and build momentum on the platforms.

Treat those first months as a ramp-up period, and judge results once that foundation is in place. Without review history or ranking, early occupancy and rates understate what your home earns once the platforms show it more often.

How Do Owner Statements and Payouts Work?

Platform payouts go straight to you. Each month we send you an owner statement backed by source documents, so you can reconcile our figures on your own.

You can check every booking and payment whenever you like and ask for records for any period, so you can verify the numbers without having to take our word for it.

Payouts go into your own accounts, and every statement line can be traced to a source document.

  1. Payouts reach your accounts

    Platform payouts go straight into your own platform accounts.

  2. Monthly statement sent

    By the 10th of the following month: total revenue, our fee, expenses and your net payout.

  3. Check every line yourself

    Each line ties to a platform payout report or a partner invoice you can check in your own accounts.

  4. Request files for accountant

    Statements, payout reports and partner invoices as CSV and PDF files within 5 business days.

  5. Discrepancies corrected

    Any discrepancy you flag within 30 days is corrected on your next statement.
